Project Manager

Detects when an AI-assisted project is circling rather than progressing. It does not do the work and will not suggest a direction. It reads, compares against the goal and the commitment ledger, and reports. Send feedback.

OpenAI

How to get a key.

Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local). Sent only to api.openai.com. Never included in exports. About 2 cents per captured round with gpt-4o, more with more out-of-scope rules.

The checks were built and tested with gpt-4o. Change only if you know why.

Checks

Project Manager only reads the assistant's replies, so work you did yourself is invisible to it. When a reply doesn't address the open commitment, check C2 shows unverifiable (a warning) instead of failing. You can confirm it with Mark executed or Close with reason. If you haven't resolved it after this many rounds, C2 fails. Default 3.

Off by default. Commitments are judged from the responses you capture; ones nothing settles are left open to age (C3) and listed under Stale in the popup and on-page panel, where you can act on them when you choose.

Projects

Projects start when you capture your first round in a conversation: the capture button asks which project it belongs to, or lets you name a new one. Manage them here. Each project has its own goal, commitment ledger and round history, and rounds are never compared across projects.

No projects yet. Click Capture round under a response on chatgpt.com or claude.ai to start one.

Stale backlog

Every commitment C3 counts as open for 2 or more rounds, oldest first, with the sentence it came from and the round that raised it. Nothing here closes by itself and no model is asked: close or defer each one with a click.

Development notes (not needed to use this)

What the author uses to measure the extension. Nothing here needs your attention.

C7

C7 (asks piling up) is counting only. It shows how many open steps are yours, the assistant's, waits, or unclear, but never warns or fails. Its old thresholds were fitted to a count that included almost every commitment, so they mean nothing now that only your own steps count. It will be refitted from real rounds in which who must act is known.

Automatic assignment (shadow mode)

On a conversation's first capture, Project Manager predicts which project it belongs to and records the prediction next to the project you choose. It acts on nothing: it never files a conversation and never writes to a ledger. A prediction needs a quote found in the conversation and a lead of at least 25 points over the runner-up (another project, or none of them); otherwise it abstains, and that is recorded too. The tally shows what automatic assignment could cover before any of it is switched on.

Event record

Every verdict rendered and every later event that bears on it (resolved, reopened, deferred, restated, undone), kept append-only so the checks' accuracy can be measured later. Events only: nothing here decides whether a check was right. Capped at 5 MB for all projects together; over the cap the oldest verdicts lose their evidence text first, and if that isn't enough recording stops and this says so.

Check accuracy

Read from the event record through a declared mapping (lib/accuracy.js), from verdicts as they were recorded at capture, never recomputed. Three outcomes: right, wrong, no outcome. A resolution nobody reopened is counted as unchallenged, never as right. Three states per check: measured by events (a claim decided through the mapping), measured by hand (your scored panels, with their source), or never tested. A check that has only ever passed is never tested, not right.

Backup

Exports every project's name, goal and ledger, plus which conversation belongs to which project. The API key is never included. Import also accepts a v0.1 export or manager.py's ledger.json, which arrives as one project.

Debug

Logs every step (page script, right-click, capture, OpenAI call) to the service worker console and to the log below, and shows a status pill in the bottom-left of chatgpt.com / claude.ai. Also adds Project Manager: show a test error to the right-click menu on those sites. API keys are masked in the log.
